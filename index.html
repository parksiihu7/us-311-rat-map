Where this map comes from

Every record is pulled by scripts/fetch_311.py from a public municipal 311 API — Socrata (NYC, Chicago, San Francisco, Cincinnati, Cambridge, Baton Rouge, New Orleans), CKAN (Boston, Pittsburgh) or ArcGIS REST (Washington DC, Baltimore, Detroit). Nothing is synthetic, modelled, or hand-entered. The script can be re-run at any time; it refetches the current year and adds new years as they appear.

Read this before comparing cities. A 311 record is a report about a place, not a rat. Chicago counts requests to bait a location; New York counts resident sightings; Washington counts inspection requests opened by DC Health. Cities also differ in how well they publicise 311, so a high count can mean more rats, more reporting, or both. This atlas is built for studying within-city patterns over time and space, not for ranking cities.

Coverage

Fields in the export

complaint_date, city, state, latitude, longitude, standardized_type, original_classification, data_source, source_dataset. Dates are ISO YYYY-MM-DD. Records without usable coordinates are excluded from the map and the CSV — their count per city is in the table above. The original classification is preserved verbatim next to the standardized one.

Limitations

  • Only cities that publish a rodent-specific 311 category with coordinates are included. Large cities that don't (Seattle, Los Angeles, Philadelphia, Dallas, Houston, Nashville and others) are documented as checked and excluded rather than approximated — see docs/DATA_SOURCES.md in the repository.
  • Baltimore's scheduled baiting runs (SW-Rat Rubout Proactive, about 95% of its rodent rows) are excluded: they are the city's work calendar, not resident reports.
  • Chicago drops requests flagged duplicate = true; the historical Chicago dataset has no such flag.
  • Coordinates are published by the city, snapped to the address or block level. Rounding to 5 decimals is applied only for file size.
  • San Francisco's rodent service detail stops in February 2024 and 28% of its rodent cases have no coordinates; Boston has no 2014 rows because its 2014 resource is empty.
  • Coverage start dates differ by city (2012–2019); see the table. The most recent day varies by city's publication lag.
